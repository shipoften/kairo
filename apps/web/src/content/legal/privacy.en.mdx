# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 28, 2026**  
**Last updated: July 28, 2026**

Welcome to Kairo (the "Platform", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information in accordance with applicable laws. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information, and the rights you may have.

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using our services.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information processing when you access or use Kairo through our website, web application, and related services, including but not limited to:

- Browsing the task board, publishing tasks, and accepting tasks
- Account registration and sign-in (including third-party OAuth)
- Wallet deposits, withdrawals, and transaction history
- Notifications, referral rewards, dispute handling, and support communications
- Admin operations (if you are an operator)

This Policy does not cover third-party websites, wallet extensions, social platforms (such as X), or services governed by their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

- **Profile data**: display name, avatar, preferred language, publish/earn mode preference, and similar settings
- **Login bindings**: identifiers from third-party accounts you choose to connect (such as X, Google, or Telegram), within the scope of your authorization
- **Business content**: task titles and descriptions, target links, review criteria, proof materials (screenshots, links, notes), and dispute statements
- **Withdrawal details**: on-chain payout addresses you submit (for example, TRC20 addresses) and related request data
- **Communications**: feedback, appeals, or support messages you send to us

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device and logs**: IP address, browser type, operating system, access time, page paths, and request identifiers
- **Security and risk signals**: session identifiers, abuse indicators, and rate-limit related records
- **Cookies and local storage**: used to maintain sign-in state, language preferences, and necessary security checks

### 2.3 Information from third parties

If you sign in with a third-party account, we may receive public profile data that the provider shares within your authorization (such as user ID, display name, or avatar). The exact scope is shown on the third-party authorization screen.

### 2.4 Public blockchain data

Deposits and withdrawals involve blockchain networks. Transaction hashes, addresses, amounts, and timestamps are publicly visible on-chain. We may read and reconcile such public data to confirm deposits, track withdrawals, and prevent duplicate crediting.

## 3. How We Use Information

We process personal information only where lawful, fair, and necessary, primarily to:

1. **Provide core services**: authentication, task publishing and joins, proof submission and review, dispute handling, and referral rewards
2. **Manage funds and ledgers**: balances, freezes, releases, settlements, deposit confirmation, withdrawals, and transaction records
3. **Protect security and integrity**: fraud prevention, anti-abuse, anti-cheat, moderation, and handling of policy violations
4. **Send notices**: task status, review outcomes, dispute updates, withdrawal results, and system announcements
5. **Improve the product**: optimize experience, performance, and features using aggregated or de-identified statistics
6. **Meet legal obligations**: respond to lawful requests from competent authorities and comply with applicable laws

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share or disclose information only in these cases:

- **With your consent or through your actions**: for example, business content you publish in tasks or submissions that is visible to the relevant counterparties
- **Service providers**: infrastructure, messaging, analytics, or security vendors that process data solely on our instructions and as needed to operate the Platform
- **Counterparty-visible information**: limited information necessary for publishers and earners to complete a task (such as task content, submitted proofs, and review feedback)
- **Legal and safety**: where required by law, or to protect users and the Platform, investigate fraud, or respond to security incidents
- **Business transfers**: in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, we will require the successor to honor this Policy and provide notice as required by law

## 5. Storage and Cross-Border Transfers

We may store and process information in locations needed to operate the Platform. Where cross-border transfers occur, we use reasonable safeguards such as contractual protections and access controls, and we comply with applicable legal requirements.

We retain information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy. After that period, we delete, anonymize, or minimize records. Accounting and dispute records may be retained longer where required by law.

## 6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to:

- Maintain signed-in sessions
- Remember language and interface preferences
- Improve security and reduce abuse

You can manage cookies in your browser settings. Disabling necessary cookies may prevent some features (such as sign-in) from working.

## 7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, encryption in transit, least-privilege access, logging, and anomaly monitoring.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your account and third-party authorizations, and never share verification secrets. Contact us immediately if you notice suspicious activity.

## 8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

- **Access and correct**: view and update profile data and preferences
- **Delete and close**: request deletion of certain data or account closure (except where retention is required by law or unsettled obligations remain)
- **Withdraw consent**: stop using related features or disconnect third-party bindings (which may affect those services)
- **Object to certain automated processing**: where applicable and where it significantly affects your rights

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. We will respond within a reasonable period and explain if we cannot fulfill a request as required by law.

## 9. Children

The Platform is intended for users with full legal capacity. If you are a minor, use the Platform only with guardian consent and guidance, and ask your guardian to review this Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if we learn that we have, we will handle it as required by law.

## 10. Third-Party Services and Links

The Platform may link to third-party websites, wallets, blockchain explorers, or social networks. Those services are operated independently and are not covered by this Policy. Please review their privacy practices before use.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. For material changes, we will provide notice through in-product notices, on-page banners, or other reasonable means. Continued use of the Platform after an update means you accept the revised Policy, unless applicable law provides otherwise.

## 12. Contact Us

If you have questions, complaints, or requests about this Policy or our handling of personal information, contact us through the Platform's in-product feedback channels or the contact methods we publish for operations. We will verify and respond as soon as reasonably possible.
